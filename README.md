## 1. Problem Understanding

### Chosen sub-problem: Follow-through

- **The gap we saw:**  
  Civic issues such as broken streetlights, potholes, major road damage, and water leakages may be reported, but reporting alone does not guarantee that the issue is assigned, repaired, and verified.

- **Why it matters:**  
  Unresolved civic issues can affect public safety, infrastructure, water resources, and the overall quality of life. Without proper follow-through, citizens may not know whether their complaint has been acted upon.

- **Why we chose this over the others:**  
  We wanted to focus on the complete journey of a civic issue rather than stopping at detection or reporting. Civic Pulse connects detection, prioritization, assignment, repair, and verification into one trackable workflow.

- **What "solved" looks like for us:**  
  Every civic issue should have a clear lifecycle:

  **Detected → Located → Prioritized → Assigned → Repaired → Verified → Closed**

  A problem should not be considered resolved simply because it was reported or assigned. It should reach verified closure.

4. How Civic Pulse Works
1. Detect
A civic issue is reported through an image or digital report.
The AI-assisted system identifies the possible issue category.

2. Locate
The system associates the issue with its geographical location.

3. Prioritize
The issue is given a priority based on factors such as:
Issue type
Severity
Public safety impact
Location
Potential number of people affected

4. Assign
The issue is routed to the appropriate maintenance team.
Example
Pothole          → Road Maintenance
Streetlight      → Electrical Maintenance
Water Leakage    → Water Supply Team

5. Repair
The assigned team works on the reported issue and updates its status.

6. Verify
Repair evidence is submitted and the issue is verified before being marked as resolved.

7. Close
Only after verification is the civic issue marked as Closed.

5. Key Idea
A civic issue is not complete when it is reported. It is complete when it is verified as resolved.
Civic Pulse focuses on the follow-through gap between reporting a problem and confirming that the problem has actually been fixed.
