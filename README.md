## 1. Problem Understanding

### Chosen sub-problem: Follow-through

- **The gap we saw:**  
  Civic issues such as broken streetlights, potholes, major road damage, and water leakages may be reported, but reporting alone does not guarantee that the issue is assigned, repaired, and verified.

- **Why it matters:**  
  Unresolved civic issues can affect public safety, infrastructure, water resources, and the overall quality of life. Without proper follow-through, citizens may not know whether their complaint has been acted upon.

- **Why we chose this over the others:**  
  We wanted to focus on the complete journey of a civic issue rather than stopping at detection or reporting. Civic Pulse connects detection, prioritization, assignment, repair, and verification into one trackable workflow.

- **What "solved" looks like for us:**  
  Every civic issue should have a clear lifecycle:

  **Detected → Located → Prioritized → Assigned → Repaired → Verified → Closed**

  A problem should not be considered resolved simply because it was reported or assigned. It should reach verified closure.

## 2. Proposed Solution
